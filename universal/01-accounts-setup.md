# 01. Developer Accounts Setup

Setting up your online developer profiles is the first step toward building your portfolio, participating in contests, collaborating with peers, and accessing compute resources.

---

## 1. GitHub Account

GitHub is where developers worldwide host, track, and collaborate on code. You will use it for college assignments, open-source projects, hackathons, and showcasing your work to recruiters.

### Step-by-Step Registration:

1. Navigate to [github.com](https://www.github.com).
2. Click **Sign up** in the upper right corner.
   
   ![Sign up on GitHub](../images/Pasted%20image%2020260904030941.png)

3. Enter your email address (preferably your personal email or college institutional email), create a strong password, and select a **professional username** (e.g., `firstnamelastname` or `firstname-dev`). Avoid unprofessional handles; you will provide this URL on your resume and LinkedIn.
   
   ![Enter details](../images/Pasted%20image%2020260904031228.png)

4. Complete the CAPTCHA verification puzzle and enter the 6-digit one-time password (OTP) sent to your email address:
   
   ![Enter OTP](../images/Pasted%20image%2020260904031555.png)

5. Complete the initial onboarding questionnaire (choose "Student").
6. **Pro Tip**: Apply for the [GitHub Student Developer Pack](https://education.github.com/pack) using your college student ID or `.ac.in` / `.edu` email address to get free access to GitHub Copilot, domain names, cloud credits, and premium developer tools!

## 2. Codeforces Account

Codeforces is the premier global platform for competitive programming, hosting rated contests multiple times each week.

### Step-by-Step Registration:

1. Visit [codeforces.com](https://codeforces.com/).
2. Click **Register** in the top right corner.
   
   ![Codeforces Register](../images/Pasted%20image%2020260904044811.png)

3. Choose a handle (username), enter your email address, and set a secure password (or register directly with your Google account).
   
   ![Codeforces Details](../images/Pasted%20image%2020260904045015.png)

4. Open your email inbox and click the activation link to confirm your account.
5. Once logged in, visit **Settings** > **Social** and add your GitHub profile and real name.

## 4. Kaggle Account

Kaggle (by Google) is the world's largest community for data science and machine learning. It provides free cloud Jupyter notebooks with **free NVIDIA T4 / P100 GPUs and TPUs** (up to 30+ hours/week), massive datasets, and community competitions.

### Step-by-Step Registration:

1. Visit [kaggle.com](https://www.kaggle.com/).
2. Click **Register** on the navigation bar (registering with Google is recommended).
   
   ![Kaggle Registration](../images/kaggle-setup-1.png)
   ![Kaggle Google Sign In](../images/kaggle-setup-2.png)

3. Review and accept the Kaggle Terms of Service and Community Guidelines:
   
   ![Kaggle Terms](../images/kaggle-setup-3.png)
   ![Kaggle Welcome Onboarding](../images/kaggle-setup-4.png)

4. Complete the introductory user onboarding:
   
   ![Kaggle Interest Selection](../images/kaggle-setup-5.png)
   ![Kaggle Experience Level](../images/kaggle-setup-6.png)
   ![Kaggle Goal](../images/kaggle-setup-7.png)
   ![Kaggle Setup Progress](../images/kaggle-setup-8.png)
   ![Kaggle Finalize Setup](../images/kaggle-setup-9.png)
   ![Kaggle Complete](../images/kaggle-setup-10.png)
   ![Kaggle Dashboard](../images/kaggle-setup-11.png)

5. **Profile & Phone Verification for Free GPUs**:
   - Navigate to your Profile > **Settings**.
   - Under **Phone Verification**, verify your mobile number. This is required by Kaggle to enable free GPU/TPU accelerator quotas and internet access in cloud notebooks.
   - Fill in your Bio, Occupation ("Student"), Organization/College name, and links to your GitHub and LinkedIn profiles:
   
   ![Kaggle Profile Overview](../images/kaggle-profile-1.png)
   ![Kaggle Profile Edit](../images/kaggle-profile-2.png)
   ![Kaggle Profile Bio](../images/kaggle-profile-3.png)
   ![Kaggle Profile Links](../images/kaggle-profile-4.png)

6. **API Token Setup (Useful for CLI & Courses)**:
   - In Settings, scroll to the **API** section and click **Create New Token**.
   - This downloads a file named `kaggle.json`. Keep it safe—it allows you to download datasets and submit models via the command line.

## 5. Hugging Face Account

Hugging Face is the central hub for modern AI, Large Language Models (LLMs), open-weights models, datasets, and AI demo hosting (Spaces).

### Step-by-Step Registration:

1. Visit [huggingface.co](https://huggingface.co/).
2. Click **Sign Up** in the top navigation bar.
   
   ![Hugging Face Signup](../images/huggingface-setup-1.png)

3. Enter your email address and choose a password:
   
   ![Hugging Face Credentials](../images/huggingface-setup-2.png)

4. Fill in your user profile:
   - **Username**: Select a clean handle.
   - **Full Name**: Your legal/professional name.
   - **GitHub username**: Link your GitHub profile.
   - **Interests**: Machine Learning, NLP, Computer Vision, etc.
   
   ![Hugging Face User Details](../images/huggingface-setup-3.png)
   ![Hugging Face Organization](../images/huggingface-setup-4.png)

5. Check your email inbox and click the verification link:
   
   ![Hugging Face Email Verification](../images/huggingface-setup-5.png)

6. Once verified, your Hugging Face profile is fully active!
   
   ![Hugging Face Verified](../images/huggingface-setup-6.png)

7. **Create a User Access Token**:
   - Click your profile avatar in the top right > **Settings** > **Access Tokens**.
   - Click **Create new token**, name it `dev-token`, and set the type to `Read`.
   - You will use this token when downloading models with Python's `transformers` or `huggingface_hub` libraries.
